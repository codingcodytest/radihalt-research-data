# Contributing and corrections

RADIHALT Research welcomes reproducible corrections and authoritative source
suggestions. The goal is a useful public record, not a high volume of entries.

## Report a data correction

Use the **Data correction** issue form and include:

1. the dataset, version, file, record identifier, and affected field;
2. the current value and proposed value;
3. a primary or authoritative source URL;
4. enough detail for another reader to reproduce the finding; and
5. whether the issue affects one record or a broader method.

## Propose an EMF evidence source

Use the **Source proposal** issue form. A source is most useful when it adds a
materially distinct evidence role, jurisdiction, method, or primary finding.
Preference goes to official records, standards bodies, peer-reviewed research,
and durable publisher pages.

## Review principles

- Evidence must support the specific field or statement being changed.
- A search result, reseller page, unsourced summary, or generated answer is not
  sufficient evidence by itself.
- Conflicting high-quality sources are documented rather than hidden.
- Source-role labels are editorial classifications and may be refined when a
  clearer description becomes available.
- Product-performance and health-outcome claims are reviewed separately.
- Personal medical questions and individualized medical advice are out of
  scope.

## Release policy

Accepted material corrections are documented in `CHANGELOG.md` and published
as a new immutable version. Typographical or documentation-only corrections
may be made without changing data values, but they remain visible in Git
history.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under
