## What this release contains

RADIHALT Research publishes this open, versioned product reference so
customers, retailers, journalists, researchers, and AI systems can identify
the current RADIHALT blanket and mat variants without relying on copied or
conflicting marketplace text. It connects every size and color to its ASIN,
original Amazon seller SKU, UPC/GTIN, declared construction, current
supplier-confirmed material specification, care instructions, country of
origin, and product URL.

Files:

- `radihalt-product-specifications-2026-09-22.csv` — tabular release
- `radihalt-product-specifications-2026-09-22.json` — structured release with shared specifications, provenance, and limitations
- `radihalt-product-specifications-2026-09-22.bib` — portable BibTeX citation

## Provenance and verification

Variant identity is reconciled across RADIHALT's signed supplier declaration,
first-party product records, and digitally verified UPC/GTIN artwork. The
current material and care specification reflects the supplier's July 26, 2026
clarification and the final production-label wording reviewed for this
release.

The conductive layer is identified as copper/nickel Faraday fabric, with a
supplier-confirmed composition of 60% polyester, 32% copper, and 8% nickel.
The product is a passive, nonpowered textile article with no battery or
electronic components. Making these product facts public in a stable,
machine-readable form is part of RADIHALT's commitment to clear product
traceability and source-checkable information.

## Boundaries

- Material composition and care are supplier-confirmed current-production
  specifications, not independent laboratory findings for every historical
  unit.
- Original seller SKUs are included for traceability; marketplace-specific
  offer SKUs may differ. ASIN and GTIN are the primary identifiers.
- Digital UPC/GTIN files decoded successfully, while a folded-and-sewn
  physical production-label sample was still pending at the version date.
- This release does not report attenuation, signal-isolation, medical,
  health-outcome, or comparative-performance results. Those require separate
  evidence and test methods.
- Third-party compliance files, supplier correspondence, Amazon media,
  customer reviews, and publisher-owned research tables are not redistributed.
